# Genre study — anime vs gaming vs tech/AI (2026-09-22)

*Method: 8-9 accounts scraped per genre (profile join date + followers, top posts all-time min 3k likes, last ~15 posts). Sources: `research/genre-study/raw/*.json`. Live data, same day.*

## The one number that matters: breakout multiple (top post likes ÷ follower count)

How far a post can travel beyond the account's own audience:

- **tech/AI: median 3.5x, max 44x.** A 1,811-follower account hit 79k likes. A 1,684-follower account (opened April 2025) hit 47k. A 2,762-follower account hit 62k. **The AI-fatigue joke wave lets nobody accounts go mega-viral.**
- **gaming: median 0.5x, max 9x.** Nezzzzooo (19k followers) hit 165k on a joke; Kizessuu (17.9k, account opened May 2025) hit 41k. Breakouts happen, but not from the smallest tier.
- **anime: median 0.1x, max 0.2x.** Established curators never exceed their follower base — anime virality is carried by the big accounts (AnimexTwts 1.6M, AniTrendz 1.2M, MangaMoguraRE 390k). Small anime accounts stay small. **Original posts are the wrong weapon for anime entry.**

## Growth timelines (joined date → today)

- **Anime curators:** ~4-6 years to 100k+ (sugoiLITE 5y→118k, AIR_News01 6y→102k, AnimetrendsLA 4y→219k, MangaMoguraRE 5y→390k).
- **Gaming:** similar (Okami13_ 8y→368k, Genki_JPN 4.5y→106k), but the fast movers are joke/art accounts (Kizessuu: 16 months→17.9k + a 41k post).
- **Tech/AI:** steady curators take years too (TheTuringPost 5y→88k), **but breakout posts need no growth at all** — the account grows AFTER the post hits.

## What boomed in each (from the same-day viral scan + these histories)

- **tech/AI:** anti-AI-saturation jokes and relatable tech failures. Cheap to make, highest ceiling right now.
- **gaming:** art shares (fan art + credit), jokes about releases, drama. Mid-tier accounts break out.
- **anime:** announcements/lore from established accounts (news-saturated), while true breakouts come from in-crowd humor/relatables — a lane that rewards personality, not a news feed.

## Verdict

1. **Post-genre #1: tech/AI** — the only lane where our account size doesn't cap the ceiling. Breakout math is 30x better than anime.
2. **Post-genre #2: gaming** — shares the exact same audience (PC/nerd core, 20-35), supports the meme/joke/art formats that are winning.
3. **Anime: keep it as a reply lane, not a post lane.** Our reply presence there is strong and the fandom is loud, but original anime posts from a small faceless account hit a news-saturated wall (median breakthrough 0.1x).

**Two genres together = yes, tech/AI + gaming.** They reinforce one identity (the tech-gamer corner of X) instead of splitting it. Adding anime posts would dilute without adding reach; adding anime REPLIES costs nothing and rides our best replying lane.

**One genre only? Tech/AI.** Fastest loop: trend drops → joke/reaction post → breakout math 30x any anime post.
