## 0. Program facts (baseline, for calibration of the X-native claims below)

- Eligibility: 18+, eligible country, Personal/Business account in good standing, active Premium /
  Premium+ / Premium Business, **500 verified followers**, **500,000 verified Home Timeline
  impressions in the last 90 days, reply impressions excluded**.
- Payout unit ("qualified impression"): unique impression from a Premium subscriber (Premium Basic,
  Premium, Premium+, Premium Business) on the **Home Timeline**, where **>=50% of the post is visible**.
  Repeat views from same account, paid/promoted, artificial and fraudulent impressions are excluded.
- Timeline: Revenue Sharing stopped accepting new creators 2026-08-07; ends 2026-09-07; OCR
  applications roll out to existing RS members from 2026-09-08. Review ~3 business days. One appeal;
  if appeal fails, reapply after 90 days (some sources say 42 days — see conflict note).
- Payouts: biweekly, $30 minimum, Stripe / X Money; first OCR payout 2026-08-28; transitioning
  creators from 2026-09-08 land ~2026-09-25.
- Daily-average math: 500,000 / 90 = **~5,556 verified HT impressions/day**.
- Sources: https://www.techi.com/x-original-content-rewards-premium-impressions/ ,
  https://www.usefastlane.ai/blog/x-original-content-rewards-program ,
  https://beyondthe.news/dossiers/x-original-content-rewards-replaces-creator-revenue-sharing ,
  https://opentweet.io/blog/x-original-content-rewards-2026 ,

  incentives had become "misaligned" and X chose to "start fresh". Source:
