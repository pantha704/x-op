BREAKING: X is doing away with their current Revenue Sharing Program. The old Revenue Sharing program is being retired on September 7 and replaced with a new “Original Content Rewards” program. Here’s the biggest difference: New Revenue Sharing Program: • The entry requirement drops dramatically to just 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users over 90 days. • You still need 500 verified followers and Premium. • But now ONLY “qualified impressions” on ORIGINAL content generate payouts. A qualified impression means: → A UNIQUE view from a Premium user → On their Home Timeline → With at least 50% of your post visible Repeated views from the same person don’t count. Paid, promoted, artificial or fraudulent impressions don’t count. And perhaps the biggest change: X is explicitly cracking down on accounts that make money by reposting other people’s content. Copied posts, downloaded/reuploaded videos, lightly edited clips, compilations and aggregated content with little original contribution will NOT generate qualified impressions. However, commentary DOES count. So if you post a news clip and actually analyze it, explain it, react to it or add your own meaningful perspective, X says that can qualify as original content. Basically, X is shifting from: “Get huge engagement and verified views” to: “Get Premium users to view content that YOU actually created or meaningfully transformed.” They are also banning engagement farming. Repeatedly telling people to like, repost, reply, bookmark or follow can jeopardize your monetization. Payouts will still happen every two weeks with a $30 minimum. Existing creators continue earning under the old system through September 7, receive their final payout around September 11, and then will have to APPLY for the new program. Original creators and people who add real commentary may benefit. Pure aggregation/repost accounts could get absolutely crushed.

The Original Content Rewards Program is far better than the old Creator Revenue Sharing Program. Let me explain to you why... Incentive structures matter for a social platform. What you reward is what you will get. For starters... in the old model, the way you qualified was completely the opposite of how you earned. People would spam replies to big accounts to farm 5M impressions for 90 days... Only to learn you then earn primarily based off your own posts. And that is exactly what they were not focusing on that whole time since they were just reply guying to get in. So they'd be frustrated that they qualified but did not earn much once they were in.. Now, you get in by 500,000 verified home timeline impressions from your OWN posts which is exactly how you earn. The old model incentivized small accounts engaging with the same big accounts. Now let's talk about those big accounts... Some of them learned that the way you earned the most is by volume posting and driving engagement (more engagement = more pay) What better way to get engagement than just ripping off proven viral content from other platforms? This is what some of them would do: Spam post multiple times an hour with content that was not theirs from an endless supply of proven viral content. That is what made them the top earners on 𝕏, netting $5 to 20K+ bi-weekly off of unoriginal content that they did not own. Incentivizing original content and not just gaming of the system is clearly a net positive for the platform. It will also improve our timelines to where we don't just see the same stuff over and over. What are your thoughts?
