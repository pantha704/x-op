**Hard numbers from the fresh scan:**

**The format hierarchy (what actually went huge):**
1. **Image-first memes** — the single biggest category. The image IS the post; text optional (≤4 words when present).
2. **First-look / set / reveal photos** — fandom spectacle, caption just states the fact.
3. **Relatable confessions** — "They had me training a 16 yr old today n I realized that im 20 yrs old working at a movie theater" (118k); "if ur child kicks my cat i will kick ur child" (86k). Identity + emotion, no news.
4. **In-crowd jokes/misreadings** — "I am NOT watching no anime called 'code gay ass'" (127k, from a small account — one perfect line = breakout).
5. **Fandom banter** — Bellingham/Romero quote-chain (76k).
6. **AI fatigue humor — THE NEW WAVE** — "at an art cafe but they got an ai menu" (77k), "AI doesn't need to be added into every single thing" (59k), "generative ai should have never been released to the public" (56k). The sentiment flipped: anti-AI-saturation jokes now outrun AI-demo hype.
7. **Art shares with credit** — Monster Hunter fan art (72k).
8. **Insider lore** — "Fujimoto's old assistants went on to make their own hit series" (41k).

**Breakout mechanic:** small accounts hit 80-130k with ONE perfect in-crowd line + image. Follower count is not the gate; the line is.

## The playbook — 7 formats, weighted

| # | Format | Recipe | Cadence |
|---|--------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Image-first meme** | funniest image in-lane, ≤4 words or none | 1/day |
| 2 | **Reaction one-liner** | news/reveal visual + ≤12-word human reaction (never a relay) | 1/day |
| 3 | **Confession** | player/fan truth everyone feels, first person | 2-3/wk |
| 4 | **Insider lore** | anime/game production fact that makes fans go "didn't know that" | 2-3/wk |
| 5 | **AI-fatigue joke** | ride the rising anti-AI-saturation wave | 2-3/wk |
| 6 | **Art share** | fan art + artist credit (gaming/anime) | 2-3/wk |
| 7 | **Banter/quote** | wild post screenshot + one-line take | opportunistic |

**Non-negotiables (unchanged, now re-verified):**
- Media ~100%. Text ≤20 words, target ~12. The image hooks; the line is the personality.
- Reaction, never relay. If it reads like a press release, kill it.
- Ride rising sentiments (AI fatigue), not fading ones (AI awe demos).
- Never re-tell a story in the same voice (Genki_JPN: 2,536 → 139).
- The "same" test: if the whole take is what the entire fandom is thinking, that's the post.
- Post into live windows: US evening (23:00-03:00 UTC) + JP evening (09:00-13:00 UTC).

## Lane weight for posts
- Anime + games/PC = core (we live there in replies; lore + confessions + art).
- AI/tech = the fatigue lane (rising, cheap to make, high ceiling).
- Experimental 10-20%: pets/relatable/life — the confession format travels everywhere.
